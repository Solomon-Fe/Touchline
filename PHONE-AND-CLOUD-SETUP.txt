TOUCHLINE MANAGEMENT - PHONE, APK AND GOOGLE CLOUD SAVE
=======================================================
Developed by Investo Deokii

The phone version and cloud saves need the game to live at a web address of
your own. GitHub Pages hosts it for free. You only do this once.

---------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 - PUT THE GAME ONLINE (GitHub Pages, free, ~10 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://github.com and create a free account (if you don't have one).
2. Click "+" (top right) > "New repository".
   - Repository name: touchline
   - Choose "Public", then "Create repository".
3. On the new repository page click "uploading an existing file".
4. Unzip Touchline-Web.zip on your PC. Open the extracted folder, select
   EVERYTHING inside it (index.html, the css and js folders, icons, sw.js,
   manifest.webmanifest) and drag it into the GitHub page. Click
   "Commit changes".
5. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" choose "main" and "/ (root)",
   then Save.
6. After 1-2 minutes your game is live at:
       https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/touchline/
   To update later, upload the new files the same way (replace them).

---------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 - INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE (Android)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Option A - quickest (no APK):
1. On the phone, open Chrome and go to your address from Part 1.
2. Tap the menu (three dots) > "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
3. Touchline Management appears with your other apps. Open it once while
   online; after that it works offline.

Option B - a real APK file you can share to other phones:
1. On a PC or phone, go to https://www.pwabuilder.com
2. Paste your game address from Part 1 and press Start.
3. Choose "Package for stores" > Android > "Generate package"
   (you can keep the default options). Download the zip.
4. Inside the zip is an .apk file. Copy it to your phone (USB cable, Bluetooth,
   WhatsApp/Telegram to yourself, or Google Drive).
5. Tap the .apk on the phone. Allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you
   opened it from when Android asks. Install.
6. Share the same .apk with any other phone the same way. Each phone starts
   with its own new careers. Open it once with internet; it then works offline.

iPhone: open your address in Safari > Share > "Add to Home Screen".

---------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3 - GOOGLE CLOUD SAVE (continue on any device with your Gmail)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Your saves go into a private, hidden folder in YOUR OWN Google Drive. Only
this game can see them. Nobody else gets access.

A) Create a Google "Client ID" (one time, free):
1. Go to https://console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Gmail.
2. Top bar: select a project > "New project". Name it "Touchline". Create.
3. Menu > "APIs & Services" > "Library". Search "Google Drive API" > Enable.
4. Menu > "APIs & Services" > "OAuth consent screen" (or "Google Auth
   Platform"):
   - User type: External. App name: Touchline Management. Add your email.
   - Under "Test users" (Audience), add your own Gmail address
     (and any other Gmail you want to play with).
5. Menu > "APIs & Services" > "Credentials" > "Create credentials" >
   "OAuth client ID":
   - Application type: Web application
   - Authorised JavaScript origins: https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io
     (no slash at the end, no /touchline)
   - Create. Copy the "Client ID" (ends with .apps.googleusercontent.com).

B) In the game (opened from your web address or the APK):
1. Settings > Cloud save > paste the Client ID > Save Client ID.
2. "Sign in with Google" > choose your Gmail > allow access.
   (While the app is in "testing", Google shows a warning that it is
   unverified. That is expected for your own private app. Click
   "Continue".)
3. "Upload this career", and tick "Upload automatically every time I save".
4. On another device: open the game, Settings (or Load career) > Cloud save >
   sign in with the same Gmail > "Continue here" next to your career.

Note: the copy installed on your PC from the zip runs from a file, and Google
does not allow sign-in there. To use cloud saves on the PC as well, open your
web address in Microsoft Edge and choose "Apps > Install this site as an app".

---------------------------------------------------------------
SAVING
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Press Save (top bar, or Ctrl+S on PC) whenever you like. A save is permanent:
  you cannot go back to an earlier point.
- The game also saves itself every 2 months of game time (Settings >
  Autosave: every month, 2 months, 6 months or off).
- An amber dot on the Save button means there is unsaved progress.
